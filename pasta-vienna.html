A proper Italian style dinner

Start with burrata and oven roasted tomatoes or vitello tonnato, share a pasta or two and finish with tiramisu. Truffle pasta and risotto can be topped with steak strips or prawns.

Pair it with an Austrian Grüner Veltliner, a Chianti Classico or one of our signature cocktails at the bar, open until midnight.

Easy to find

On Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz on the Ringstrasse, opposite Stadtpark. St. Stephen's Cathedral is a ten minute walk along Wollzeile and the U3 Stubentor metro station is right outside.

Good to know

Where can I eat good pasta in Vienna's city centre?

At VIENERO, Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz 3, 1010 Vienna, by Stadtpark and about ten minutes on foot from St. Stephen's Cathedral. Try the pasta al tartufo with shaved black truffle.

Do you have vegetarian pasta?

Yes. Pasta al tartufo, pasta alle zucchine e pistacchio, the ratatouille lasagne and the wild mushroom risotto are all meat and fish free.

Is there an English menu?

Yes, our full menu is available in English and our team speaks English.

Do I need a reservation?
