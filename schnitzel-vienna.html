Viennese classics with a Mediterranean twist

At VIENERO, traditional Viennese cooking meets the flavours of the Mediterranean. Start with vitello tonnato, burrata with oven roasted tomatoes or our hand cut beef tartare. Finish with a chocolate soufflé and vanilla ice cream, then stay for a signature cocktail at the bar, open until midnight.

Easy to find

We are on Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz on the Ringstrasse, right opposite Stadtpark with its famous golden Johann Strauss statue. St. Stephen's Cathedral is a ten minute walk along Wollzeile, and the U3 Stubentor metro station is right outside. Open every day, kitchen until 10 pm.

Good to know

Where can I eat a good Wiener Schnitzel in Vienna's city centre?

At VIENERO, Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz 3, 1010 Vienna, right by Stadtpark and about ten minutes on foot from St. Stephen's Cathedral. Our veal Wiener Schnitzel costs 29.90 euro.

Is it made with veal?

Yes. A real Wiener Schnitzel is veal, and ours is tender veal in a light, souffléed crust, served with parsley potatoes and lingonberry jam on request.

Do I need a reservation?

We recommend booking, especially for dinner and weekends. You can book online in about a minute or call +43 1 913 70 05.

Do you have an English menu?

Yes, our full menu is available in English.
