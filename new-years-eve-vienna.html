Celebrate in the old town

Oysters and caviar to begin, Bouillabaisse Royale with lobster, then beef fillet Rossini, turbot, saddle of veal or black truffle risotto, and the Golden Midnight soufflé to finish. A DJ sets the mood, the bar mixes cocktails, and at midnight we raise a glass of champagne together.

We stay open until 2 am. Afterwards you are steps away from the city centre, and the U3 Stubentor station is right at the door. We have an English menu and a team that is happy to help.

Questions

What is included on New Year's Eve at VIENERO?

A festive menu with amuse bouche, starter, soup, a choice of main course and dessert, music with a DJ and a glass of champagne at midnight.

How much is New Year's Eve at VIENERO?

169 euros per person for the menu, DJ included. Dinner is served from 5 pm to 10 pm, then we celebrate with a DJ until 2 am.

Are you open on New Year's Day?

Yes, on 1 January 2027 we are open from 5 pm.

Is there a vegetarian main course?

Yes, the black truffle risotto with sautéed wild mushrooms and Parmesan.

Where is VIENERO?

At Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz 3 by the Stadtpark in Vienna's old town, with the U3 Stubentor station at the door and about ten minutes on foot from St. Stephen's Cathedral.

How do I book?

Email Reservierung@vienero.at or call +43 1 913 70 05. Seats are limited, so we recommend booking early.
