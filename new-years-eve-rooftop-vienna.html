Into the New Year above the Vienna skyline

The Justizcafé sits at the very top of the Palace of Justice, one of the grand buildings on the Ringstrasse. From here you look out over the rooftops and domes of the city, City Hall, Parliament and the lights of the old town. At midnight the fireworks light up all of Vienna, and you raise your glass right where the view is best.

Gillardeau oysters with red wine balsamic shallot vinaigrette to open, then beef, salmon and mushroom tartare, a truffle Parmesan soup with fresh truffle, then truffle gnocchi with black tiger prawns, beef steak skewers or salmon on saffron risotto. To finish, chocolate soufflé with Bourbon vanilla ice cream and a glass of champagne.

Dinner is cooked and served by the VIENERO team.

Then the party starts

After dessert, dinner turns into a party. The DJ takes over, the entertainment begins, and at midnight everyone raises a glass while the fireworks light up Vienna below. Dance until 4 am high above the Ringstrasse, with only 150 guests.

If you prefer our restaurant by the Stadtpark, see New Year's Eve at VIENERO.

Questions

What is included in the 399 euros?

An amuse gueule of Gillardeau oysters, the 4-course dinner with a choice of main course, a glass of champagne, mineral water, and DJ and entertainment until 4 am. Other drinks are charged separately.

What time does the evening start?

Arrival is from 6:30 pm to 9 pm, then dinner is served. The party with DJ and entertainment goes on until 4 am.

Where is the Justizcafé?

Inside the Palace of Justice at Schmerlingplatz 10-11, 1010 Vienna, at the very top on the 5th floor, reached by lift.

Who cooks at the Justizcafé?

The VIENERO team. Kitchen and service come from our restaurant by the Stadtpark.

How do I book?

Use the Reserve your seat button: choose 1 to 4 guests, enter your main courses and pay online by card, Klarna or eps. Your booking is confirmed immediately and is non-refundable. For groups of 5 or more, please email Reservierung@vienero.at or call +43 1 913 70 05.

Is there also a New Year's Eve dinner at VIENERO by the Stadtpark?

Yes, VIENERO at Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz has its own New Year's Eve menu with a DJ. All details are on the page New Year's Eve at VIENERO.
