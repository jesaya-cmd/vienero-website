Restaurant near St. Stephen's Cathedral

Looking for a restaurant near St. Stephen's Cathedral that is not a tourist trap? VIENERO is a short ten minute walk away along Wollzeile, right by Stadtpark on the Ringstrasse. Viennese classics meet the Mediterranean: Wiener Schnitzel, fresh pasta with black truffle, steaks and whole sea bass from the grill, plus cocktails until midnight.

A perfect stop on your Vienna walk

  • St. Stephen's Cathedral: about ten minutes on foot along Wollzeile, one of the city's oldest shopping streets.
  • Stadtpark: right across the street, with the golden Johann Strauss statue and the Kursalon.
  • MAK, Museum of Applied Arts: a few minutes along the Stubenring.
  • Ringstrasse: we sit on the grand boulevard, ideal before or after a tram ride around the Ring.
  • Getting here: U3 Stubentor is right outside our door.

Lunch, dinner and drinks

We are open every day: Monday to Saturday from noon, Sunday from 5 pm. The kitchen serves until 10 pm and the bar stays open until midnight. On weekdays there is a business lunch from 12 to 3 pm with seasonal soup and a main course for 15.90 euro.

Our English menu covers everything from vitello tonnato and calamari fritti to pasta al tartufo, ribeye steak and crème brûlée.
