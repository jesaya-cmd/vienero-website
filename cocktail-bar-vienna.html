Not drinking?

Easy to find

On Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz on the Ringstrasse opposite Stadtpark, ten minutes on foot from St. Stephen's Cathedral, U3 Stubentor right outside. Kitchen until 10 pm, bar until midnight.

Good to know

Where is a good cocktail bar in Vienna's city centre?

At VIENERO, Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz 3, 1010 Vienna, by Stadtpark. The bar is open every day until midnight.

Can I come just for drinks?

Yes, you are welcome at the bar for cocktails, wine or a glass of Champagne without dinner.

Do you have alcohol free cocktails?

Yes, three alcohol free cocktails plus homemade organic lemonades.

Can I book a table for drinks?

Yes, book online or call +43 1 913 70 05, especially for groups and weekends.
