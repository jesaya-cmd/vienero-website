Everyone at one table

While some order steak or Wiener Schnitzel, others share burrata, truffle wild broccoli and a truffle pasta. Add a homemade organic lemonade with no added sugar or a glass of wine.

Let us know when you book if you eat vegan or gluten free, our kitchen is happy to help.

Easy to find

On Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz opposite Stadtpark, ten minutes on foot from St. Stephen's Cathedral, U3 Stubentor right outside.

Good to know

Where can I eat vegetarian in Vienna's city centre?

At VIENERO, Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz 3, 1010 Vienna: burrata, goat cheese salad, pasta al tartufo, ratatouille lasagne and wild mushroom risotto.

Just ask when you book or at the table, our kitchen will tell you which dishes can be made vegan.
