Good to know

Where can I get a good steak in Vienna's city centre?

At VIENERO, Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz 3, 1010 Vienna. We grill ribeye (300 g) and fillet steak (200 or 250 g).

Do you serve surf and turf?

Yes, both steaks are available with grilled prawns.

Are sides included?

Sides are ordered separately, for example truffle Parmesan fries, rosemary potatoes or grilled vegetables.
