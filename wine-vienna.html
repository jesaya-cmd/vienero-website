By the glass

Grüner Veltliner, Riesling, Gemischter Satz, Sauvignon Blanc, Gelber Muskateller and Chardonnay, plus Pinot Noir, red blends and rosé, from 7.50 euro a glass. Champagne by the glass from 18 euro.

Discover Austria

Grüner Veltliner from Pichler in the Wachau and Hirsch in the Kamptal, Sauvignon Blanc from Tement in Styria, Wiener Gemischter Satz from the Nussberg vineyard in Vienna, and Blaufränkisch from Burgenland. Ask our team for a recommendation.

And the classics

Chablis Premier Cru, Sancerre, Saint Émilion and Château Montrose, Chianti Classico and Brunello di Montalcino, Joseph Phelps Insignia from Napa, and Dom Pérignon Vintage 2017 for the big moments.

Good to know

Where can I drink good wine in Vienna's city centre?

At VIENERO, Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz 3, 1010 Vienna, by Stadtpark. Wine by the glass from 7.50 euro, bar open every day until midnight.

Can I come just for a glass of wine?

Of course. Stop by the bar for a glass or a bottle, with cheese or antipasti if you like.

What is Gemischter Satz?

A Viennese white wine made from several grape varieties grown together in the same vineyard. We pour one from the Nussberg in Vienna.

Do you serve Champagne by the glass?

Yes, Champagne brut from 18 euro and rosé Champagne at 22 euro a glass.
