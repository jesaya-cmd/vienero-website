Questions and answers

Everything you want to know before your visit to VIENERO by the Stadtpark in Vienna's first district.

Where is VIENERO?

At Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz 3 in 1010 Vienna, right at the Stadtpark where Wollzeile meets the Ring, about 10 minutes on foot from St. Stephen's Cathedral. The U3 Stubentor station is at the door.

What are your opening hours?

Daily noon to midnight, from 1 February 2027 daily 4 pm to midnight. The kitchen is open until 10 pm, from February until 11 pm, the bar until midnight. Open every day.

How can I book a table?

Online in about one minute below, by phone on +43 1 913 70 05 or by email to Reservierung@vienero.at. For groups of ten or more please email us, all details are on our group page.

Can I come without a reservation?

Yes, walk-ins are always welcome. In the evening and at weekends we still recommend booking so your table is guaranteed.

What are your reviews like?

Our guests rate us 4.4 out of 5 stars from more than 1,500 reviews on Google.

Do you serve real Wiener Schnitzel?

Yes, made from veal in a light, puffed crust with parsley potatoes. More on our schnitzel page, including our Schnitzel Menu for €40.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Yes, for example burrata with roasted tomatoes, goat cheese salad, pasta with zucchini and pistachio, ratatouille lasagne and wild mushroom risotto.

Do you have vegan dishes?

Yes, we have vegan dishes and organic dishes. Our team is happy to tell you what is available vegan today.

Is VIENERO good for children?

Yes. There is a kids menu and high chairs, and you can easily come in with a stroller.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, dogs are very welcome.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. You can pay cash or with all common cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Maestro, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Is there free WiFi?

Yes, free WiFi for all guests.

Is VIENERO wheelchair accessible?

Unfortunately not fully: there are steps at the entrance and no accessible toilet. Strollers get in easily.

Do you have outdoor seating?

Yes, we have a terrace on Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz.

Can I host an event or a group dinner?

Yes, for up to 70 guests in the whole restaurant or in a separate area, with a set menu. We arrange a DJ, a host and flowers on request. All details on our group dining page.

Do you deliver?

No, we do not deliver and we are not on any delivery app. Our food is only served at our restaurant by the Stadtpark.

Is there an English menu?

Yes, our full menu is available in English, with photos of the dishes.

Is there a cover charge?

Yes, the cover charge is €3.90 per person.

Was this restaurant called Le Pic before?

Yes. Le Pic by the Stadtpark is now VIENERO: same address, same hosts, new name.
