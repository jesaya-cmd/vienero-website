Questions about catering

From how many guests does VIENERO cater?

We cater events from 50 guests. For smaller groups the best option is our restaurant, with up to 70 guests and exclusive use of the whole venue.

What does the catering include?

You choose what you need: food as finger food, flying dinner, gala dinner or buffet, drinks, service staff, decoration and entertainment such as a DJ, live music or a photo booth.

Do you organise decoration?

Yes. Flowers, table decoration, candles and lighting, balloons or festive decoration to suit your occasion.

How much does catering cost?

Every catering is put together to your wishes. Send us the date, number of guests and location and we usually reply with an offer within 24 hours.

Which occasions is the catering for?

Company events, client events, summer parties, galas, weddings, birthdays and Christmas parties from 50 guests.
