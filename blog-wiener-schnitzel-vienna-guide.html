Wiener Schnitzel in Vienna: what to know before you order

Blog · Vienna food guide · 18 October 2026

VIENERO by the Stadtpark at dusk. Photo: Raphael Fasching

No visit to Vienna is complete without a Wiener Schnitzel. But menus can be confusing, and not every golden cutlet is the real thing. Here is a short guide so you know exactly what you are ordering.

Veal is the original

In Austria, a dish may only be called Wiener Schnitzel if it is made from veal. If a menu says Schnitzel Wiener Art, it is usually pork. Both can be delicious, but if you want the classic, look for veal.

How a great Schnitzel looks

The meat is pounded thin, coated in flour, egg and breadcrumbs and fried until golden. The best ones have a breadcrumb coat that puffs up and rises away from the meat in soft waves. In Vienna this is called souffliert, and it is the sign of a Schnitzel fried with care.

What to eat with it

Traditional sides are parsley potatoes or a Viennese potato salad, a wedge of lemon and often lingonberry jam. A glass of Austrian white wine such as Grüner Veltliner goes perfectly with it.
