Company events, team dinners and client evenings in Vienna

An evening your team will talk about. VIENERO is right by the Stadtpark in Vienna's 1st district, with the U3 Stubentor station outside the door and the Hilton Vienna Park a two minute walk away. We host company groups from 10 guests and up to 70 with exclusive use of the whole venue, with Wiener Schnitzel, pasta from our own pasta workshop, dry aged steak and a bar open until midnight.

Questions about company events

How many guests can VIENERO host for a company event?

We host company groups from 10 guests. Two adjoining rooms seat up to 50 guests for your group only, and with exclusive use of the whole restaurant and bar we host up to 70.

How much is a company dinner?

Our three course group menu is 60 euros per person, excluding drinks. We put together drinks packages to suit you.

Can you organise a DJ, a host and decoration?

Yes. We organise a DJ, a host or moderator, flowers, table decoration, a sparkling wine reception and more on request.

How does payment work?

We work with prepayment. After confirmation you receive a secure electronic payment link.

When do you need the menu choices?

For groups of 20 or more we ask for the final menu choices 10 days before the event.

How quickly do we get an offer?

Send us the date, time, number of guests and occasion. We usually reply with a tailored offer within 24 hours.

Where is VIENERO?

At Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz 3, 1010 Vienna, right by the Stadtpark. The U3 Stubentor station is outside the door and the Hilton Vienna Park is a two minute walk.
