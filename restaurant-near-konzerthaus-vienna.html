A short walk to the show

The Ronacher on Seilerstätte is about six minutes on foot. The Konzerthaus on Lothringerstraße and the Akademietheater on Lisztstraße are around ten minutes away, the Musikverein about twelve. The U3 Stubentor station is right outside our door.

Our tip for the evening

If your performance starts at 7:30 pm, book for around 5:30 pm. Tell our team when you arrive what time you need to leave and we will plan the evening with you. The kitchen is open daily until 10 pm, the bar until midnight.

Questions about dinner before a concert

How far is VIENERO from the Konzerthaus?

From Dr.-Karl-Lueger-Platz 3 it is about a ten minute walk to the Wiener Konzerthaus on Lothringerstraße, and about six minutes to the Ronacher theatre on Seilerstätte.

When should I book before a concert?

For a 7:30 pm start we suggest a table at around 5:30 pm. That leaves time for a main course and dessert. Just tell our team when you need to leave.

Can I come after the show?

Yes. The kitchen is open daily until 10 pm and the bar until midnight, perfect for a drink or a glass of wine after the concert.

How do I book?

Online on our booking page or by phone on +43 1 913 70 05.
